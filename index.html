A letter from Deinunlhing

Dear Madam Rani,

Happy Teacher's Day! ❤️

I may not always say it out loud, but I am truly grateful to have you as my teacher. You have taught us much more than Social Science and Hindi. Through your classes, your patience, your explanations, and even those moments when we were a little too noisy, you have given us lessons that will stay with us.

Thank you for explaining things again when we didn't understand, for encouraging us when we doubted ourselves, and for believing that we could do better even when our notebooks seemed to disagree with that idea.

Your classes have given us knowledge, but your kindness has given us confidence. That is something a textbook can never teach.

I hope you know how much you are appreciated. Every lesson you teach, every correction you make, every smile you share, and every little moment you spend helping us becomes part of our school memories.

So today, I simply want to say: Thank you for being an amazing teacher, Madam Rani.

With heartfelt gratitude,
Deinunlhing Kholhou
Don Bosco High School
